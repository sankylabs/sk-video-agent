Qualification Checklist for Potential Memberships (Via Any lead source)
For Leads Interested in Camps:
✅ Refer to Website → Direct them to our Camps page to explore available camps and book directly through the website.
✅ If They Are Unsure About Our Academy or Willing to Travel → Offer a tour of the academy to help them get a feel for our space and program.
✅ During the Tour → If they seem interested in more than just camps and meet membership criteria, upsell membership options (at least Light membership).
✅ Follow Up After Tour → Send a follow-up email with any applicable information/offers and maintain contact to potentially convert to membership
For Leads Interested in Memberships: 
Validate if They Are a Qualified Lead:
✅ Age Check: Ensure they are 5-14 years old. Check if they have done STEM activities before as well. 
✅ Location Check:
* Are you located in Kirkland? → Proceed with membership discussion.
* If Clyde hill/ Woodinville → Slight chances that they prefer kirkland, so double check on their commute limitations to kirkland ( once or twice a week )
* If other locations or far away→ Suggest they compare our location to their nearest alternative.
✅ Commute Feasibility:
* Can they commute to the Kirkland academy at least once or twice a week if they sign up for a membership?
✅ Interest & Background Check:
* How did you find out about us? (Referral, Instagram, passing by, organic search, etc.)
* Which school does your child attend? (Public, Private, Homeschool)
* What other activities is your child involved in? (to understand how packed is their schedule?)
* Can they commit at least once or twice a week if they enjoy our program?( Kids need to build a momentum to build future ready skills)
* Validate if the Parent Tech Savvy?( 
* Validate indirectly if they can afford this program (look for the clues : private school means YES for affordability, if both parents are working in big tech and too busy)
